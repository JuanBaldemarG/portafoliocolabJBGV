Several passengers on board a commercial flight operated by Precision Air that crashed into Lake Victoria in Tanzania were rescued after emergency teams reached the site near Bukoba airport. The aircraft had departed from Dar es Salaam and encountered trouble while approaching its destination. Local authorities said rescue boats, airport personnel, and nearby residents participated in efforts to pull survivors from the partially submerged plane while weather conditions remained difficult.

Officials reported that dozens of people were on the flight, including passengers and crew. Early statements indicated that several survivors had been taken to a regional hospital for treatment while investigators worked to determine the exact cause of the accident. Videos shared by witnesses showed the aircraft tilted in the water as responders coordinated around the fuselage. Regional leaders asked the public to remain calm and allow emergency crews to continue their work without interference.

The accident immediately raised questions about aviation safety and weather-related risk in the area. Authorities said a formal investigation would review communications with the cockpit, the landing approach, and the mechanical condition of the aircraft. The airline expressed sympathy for the families affected and said it would cooperate fully with investigators. National leaders also offered condolences and called for support for the victims and their relatives as more confirmed information became available.
